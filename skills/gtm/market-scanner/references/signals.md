# Signals: how many companies are showing one right now

A signal is something a company did recently that gives the member a good reason to reach out now. It is never proof that the company will buy.

## Pick 3 to 5 signals that fit what the member sells

Read `## What I sell` and `## Who I help` in `ACX/my-business.md`. Pick signals where the link to the offer is obvious. Explain the link in one line in the report.

| Signal | Plain meaning | Fits offers like |
|---|---|---|
| Hiring for a role | Posted a job for the kind of work the member does or supports | Tools or services that help that team, or replace that hire |
| Active on social video | Posting short-form video on TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts or LinkedIn video | Content, video, social and creator tools |
| Raised money | Announced a funding round | Most B2B offers: new budget and growth targets |
| New leader | Hired a new head of marketing, sales or growth | Offers that new leaders buy in their first 90 days |
| Launched something | Released a product or feature | Marketing, content and launch support |
| Growing team | Headcount up sharply in the last 6 months | Offers that help growing teams |
| Uses a related tool | A tool linked to the offer shows on their website | Integrations, replacements, add-ons |

## How to count each signal (best method first)

1. **Prospeo signal filter (Counted).** Use the filters in `prospeo-company-search.md`. Same company filters as the main count, plus one signal filter. One credit each, only after approval. This is the cleanest count because it uses the same target.
2. **Directory or job-board total (Counted).** A public page that shows a total, for example a job board's result count for `"video editor" SaaS` in the target countries. Write down the exact query, the filters and the number shown. Note that it may include companies outside the target.
3. **Web sample, scaled up (Sampled).** When there is no total:
   - Take a fair sample of the target companies (for example the 25 from the Prospeo first page, or the named list from the web estimate).
   - Check each one for the signal by hand (visit its social profiles, careers page, or news).
   - Count how many show it, for example `7 of 25 = 28%`.
   - Scale up: `28% × [Prospeo cleaned range]`, shown as a range.
   - Label it **Sampled** and give the sample size.

## For every signal, record

- the count and its label (Counted or Sampled)
- the time window (last 30, 60 or 90 days)
- the exact method or query
- up to 5 example companies with a link to the evidence (the job post, the video, the article)

## Overlap

When the same list of companies was checked for several signals (as in a web sample), report how many show two or more. List them by name. These are the warmest accounts and should be the first action in the report.

## Never

- Count a signal you did not see evidence for.
- Use a signal older than the window you stated.
- Look up people, emails or phone numbers. Signal Watcher does that.
