# Web estimate: how many companies could buy

The goal is one honest range, not a precise number. The member must be able to follow the working and check every source.

## Where to look (use at least three kinds)

| Kind of source | What it gives | Example searches |
|---|---|---|
| Software directories | Number of listed products in a category | `G2 "[category]" software`, `Capterra [category]`, `Product Hunt [category]` |
| Market maps and landscape reports | Named companies in a space, sometimes a total | `[category] market map 2026`, `[category] landscape` |
| Industry reports and statistics | Total companies in an industry or country | `number of SaaS companies in [country]`, `how many [industry] companies` |
| Public company databases | Counts shown on public pages | `Crunchbase [category] companies`, `Tracxn [category]` |
| Credible articles | Totals quoted from research | Prefer sources that name where their number comes from |

Rules:

- Only use a number you can see on the page. Write down the exact number and what it counts.
- Prefer the most recent source. Note the year of each number.
- Do not use a number when the page does not say what it counts.
- If a source counts a much wider group (all SaaS companies), apply a share and show the share and why.

## How to build the range

1. Write each source as one line: `Source → what it counts → number`.
2. Adjust each one to the target (country, size, type). Show every adjustment, for example `≈ 40% are in our countries (source: ...)`.
3. Take the lowest adjusted number as **low** and the highest believable one as **high**. Drop any source that is far outside the others, and say why.
4. Compare with the Prospeo count. If the web range is far below Prospeo's count, the Prospeo filters are probably too loose. If it is far above, Prospeo is probably missing companies. Say which.
5. Set confidence:
   - **High:** three or more sources agree within about 2×.
   - **Medium:** sources differ by 2× to 5×, or one key adjustment is a guess.
   - **Low:** fewer than three usable sources, or they differ by more than 5×.

## Companies that clearly fit

List 10 to 15 real companies found during the research. For each, link the company's own website and write one line on why it fits. Never list a company you have not seen on the web in this session.
