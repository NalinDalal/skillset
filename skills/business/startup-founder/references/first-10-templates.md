# First 10: message, Loom, and quote templates

Use with section 3 of `gtm-funnel.md`. At this stage there is no sequence. One message per person, one Loom per account, one file of their words.

## The one-paragraph message

Shape: observation, work already done, small ask.

```text
Saw {specific thing they did}, {detail that proves you looked}.

{What you already built for them}. {What it shows, in one clause}.

Want me to send it over?
```

Rules:

- The first clause names something only they would recognize. If the message could go to 50 people unchanged, rewrite it.
- The work is already done. Not "I could build", but "I built".
- One ask. A yes or no question beats a calendar link at this stage.
- No greeting line, no company intro, no signature block past your name.
- 60 to 90 words. Long enough to carry the observation, short enough to read on a phone.

## Three openers, by signal

| Signal | Opener | Why it works |
|---|---|---|
| Comment under a competitor's post | "Saw your comment on {competitor}'s post about {topic}. You said {their phrase}." | Quotes them back to themselves. |
| Just raised | "Congrats on the {round}. Most {role}s at that stage hit {specific problem} in the first 90 days." | Ties the news to the pain, not to the money. |
| Public complaint | "You wrote that {quote}. That is the exact thing {product} removes." | Their words, not yours. |

Then the same second half: the thing you already built, and the yes or no ask.

## Fill-in block

```text
Signal:             {competitor comment | raise | complaint | other}
Their exact words:  "{quote}"
What I already did: {account built | Loom recorded | audit run | sample pulled}
One-line finding:   {what it shows}
Ask:                {send it over? | 10 minutes? | want the link?}
```

## Loom checklist

Record 90 seconds per account, and only when you have something real to show.

Before:

- Their account, repo, or sample already set up. Never a blank template.
- One gap visible on screen. Zoom so it reads at 720p.
- No other customer's data anywhere in frame.
- Their name and company on screen, so they know it is theirs.

During, 90 seconds:

1. **0:00 to 0:10.** Name the account and the finding. "This is {company}'s setup, and there is one gap in {area}."
2. **0:10 to 0:35.** Show the gap where it lives, not in a slide.
3. **0:35 to 1:10.** Show the fix applied. If you already applied it, say so.
4. **1:10 to 1:30.** One ask. "Want me to leave this in place, or walk you through it?"

After:

- Send the link in the same thread as the message. Not a new thread.
- Log the send in the tracker below.
- If they reply with a problem, fix it before the next touch.

## Quote file

One append-only file, for every customer phrase worth reusing.

| Their words, verbatim | Date | Context | Asset it feeds |
|---|---|---|---|
| | | | |

Rules:

- Verbatim. Do not tidy the grammar. The odd phrasing is what makes it land.
- One row per quote, even when a similar row exists.
- The asset column is the point: landing page, cold email, pitch deck, ad. A quote with no asset is a note.
- Read the file before you write any page copy.

## What not to send

- "I came across your profile."
- "Quick question" with no question.
- A 200-word company intro.
- A calendar link in the first message.
- Anything you could send to 50 people unchanged.
- A Loom of a demo account.
