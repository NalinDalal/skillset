# Legal & Registration (India)

## When to register at all
Do not register until you have a paying customer or a co-founder needing formal equity. Do not register until you are about to raise money, sign a real contract, or open a business bank account. Before that, build and validate as an individual. There is no need to spend money on incorporation for an unvalidated idea.

## Entity types
- **Sole Proprietorship**: fastest, no separate legal entity, unlimited personal liability. Fine for freelancing or service work under one person. This fits the current nerdev studio model. It just needs GST registration (if turnover crosses the threshold) and a current account.
- **LLP (Limited Liability Partnership)**: liability protection, cheaper compliance than Pvt Ltd. Works if 2+ founders. Works if you have no plan to raise VC money. VCs generally do not invest in LLPs.
- **Private Limited Company (Pvt Ltd)**: the standard choice if you plan to raise funding or hire formally. Also the choice if you want limited liability plus credibility with enterprise clients. Required structure for most Indian VC/angel investment.

## Pvt Ltd registration: rough steps
1. Get Digital Signature Certificate (DSC) for all directors.
2. Apply for Director Identification Number (DIN).
3. Reserve the company name through the MCA RUN/SPICe+ service.
4. File SPICe+ (integrated form: incorporation, PAN, TAN, EPFO, ESIC, GST registration all in one).
5. Draft MOA (Memorandum) and AOA (Articles of Association).
6. Get Certificate of Incorporation from MCA.
7. Open a company bank account, get PAN/TAN linked.
Realistic timeline: 1-2 weeks if documents are ready. Cost: roughly ₹6,000-15,000 in government + professional fees for a simple 2-founder company. For a first-timer, use a CA or a service like Vakilsearch/IndiaFilings/ClearTax. Do not do it yourself (DIY).

## GST
- Mandatory once turnover crosses ₹20 lakh (services) / ₹40 lakh (goods) in most states. Lower thresholds in some special-category states.
- Needed earlier if selling to businesses that require GST invoices, or selling across state lines in some cases.
- Freelance/dev services to foreign clients: check "export of services" GST rules (usually zero-rated, but registration + LUT filing still required above threshold).

## Startup India recognition (DPIIT)
- Apply after incorporation via the Startup India portal.
- Benefits: income tax exemption (3 of the first 10 years, subject to conditions) and easier compliance. Also access to Startup India Seed Fund Scheme and fast-track patent examination.
- Requirements broadly: incorporated <10 years ago, turnover <₹100 crore, working on innovation/improvement of products or a scalable business model.

## Co-founder agreements
- Even for 2 friends starting together: write a founders' agreement. Cover the equity split, vesting (standard: 4 years, 1-year cliff), and roles. Add IP assignment: all IP built for the company belongs to the company, not the individual. Also cover what happens if someone leaves early.
- Do this before writing significant product code together. It is cheap to do early and expensive or painful to fix later.

## Notes
- Rules and thresholds change. Verify current numbers before relying on the exact figures here. Check GST thresholds, Startup India criteria, and fees via a web search or a CA.
