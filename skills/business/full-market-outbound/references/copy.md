# Copy

## Rules every script follows

- Under 65 words, ideally under 45.
- Subject line of 1 to 3 words, lowercase, spintaxed 3 ways.
- Written like a message to a coworker.
- Spintax on every sentence.
- A soft CTA ("Worth a look?") in place of a meeting ask.
- Social proof from the same industry, or none at all.

A model drafts, a human checks every line, and nothing ships unchecked.

```text
{quick question|one thing|worth a look}
```

## Personalization does not pay

We ran AI-written personal first lines against emails with nothing but a first-name variable.

| Version | Reply rate |
|---|---|
| AI-written personal intro line | 0.27% |
| First-name variable only | 10%+ |

The relevance comes from the list and the offer. Add zero personalization unless it amplifies the offer. A tight segment makes one script fit thousands of people.

## The Eden email: 4 moves

```text
{{how the pain starts, in the buyer's own words}}
{{what the system does, and what it produces}}
want me to build one from a job you just finished?
Worth a look?
```

No personalization anywhere in it. The buyer recognizes the situation, sees the output, and gets an offer that costs nothing to accept.

## Google and Microsoft need different emails

| | Google | Microsoft |
|---|---|---|
| Length | Forgiving, 45 to 65 words works | 15 to 40 words |
| Greeting | Optional | None |
| Signature | Optional | None |
| Tone | Message to a coworker | Note to someone who already knows you |
| Pitch | Soft CTA | None. The ask is implicit. |

Run them as separate campaigns with separate copy. One script for both underserves the Microsoft half.

## Sequence shape

1. Fresh first email.
2. One threaded nudge, 3 days later.
3. Then a new campaign with a new angle.

A new angle is a new campaign, not a third step. A finished campaign comes back about a month later as fresh, against the same contacts, with a new subject line.

## Frameworks

**One-line free offer.**

```text
{First name}, interested in {free work} for {company}? P.S. {one line of proof}
```

**Lead magnet plus case study.**

```text
{First name}, created a {playbook} for {company} covering how we helped {client}
get {result} in {timeframe}. Mind if I share it?
```

**Pain point plus solution.**

```text
{First name}, {question about a pain they feel}? Our {solution} {fixes it} so you
{result}. Worth a look?
```

Launch 15 to 30 variants across 3 to 5 value props. Give each 1,000 sends, then cut the bottom two thirds.

## What to cut on sight

- Any sentence that names your product before it names their problem.
- Adjectives doing the work of proof.
- A meeting ask on the first touch.
- A second sentence that adds a second variable.
- A subject line with a number, an emoji, or title case.
- Personalization that does not amplify the offer.
