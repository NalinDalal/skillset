# SOP: Design Style Catalog

## What this skill owns

A catalog of design styles. Use it to pick a direction before building. Pair it with ui/ui-theme-picker for tokens and ui/refero-design for references.

## How to use this catalog

1. Name the product, audience, and tone.
2. Pick two or three candidate styles from the list.
3. Check the "avoid" notes to rule out bad fits.
4. Lock one style and stay consistent.

## The styles

### Minimalism

Less is more. Whitespace, limited colour, strong typography.

Best for: SaaS, tools, portfolios. Avoid when the brand needs warmth or storytelling.

### Maximalism

More is more. Dense layouts, bold colour, layered elements.

Best for: creative studios, magazines, events. Avoid for utility products.

### Swiss design

Grid-based, sans-serif, objective, clean.

Best for: documentation, data products, agencies. Avoid for playful brands.

### Brutalism

Raw, unpolished, high contrast, default fonts.

Best for: portfolios, indie tools, statements. Avoid for enterprise trust.

### Surrealism

Dreamlike, unexpected imagery, distorted scale.

Best for: art, fashion, experimental brands. Avoid for functional products.

### Neo-brutalism

Brutalism with colour and polish. Thick borders, flat shadows.

Best for: indie SaaS, creator tools. Avoid for conservative buyers.

### Neo-classical

Classical proportions with modern execution.

Best for: luxury, finance, heritage brands. Avoid for tech-forward products.

### Neumorphism

Soft extruded shapes, same-colour shadows.

Best for: small controls, niche apps. Avoid for accessibility and contrast.

### Scrapbook

Collage, torn edges, handwritten notes, layered paper.

Best for: personal brands, communities. Avoid for corporate sites.

### Glassmorphism

Frosted glass panels, blur, transparency.

Best for: hero sections, overlays. Avoid as a full-page style.

### Claymorphism

Soft 3D clay-like shapes, pastel colours.

Best for: playful apps, children's products. Avoid for serious brands.

### Bento grid

Modular card grid with varied sizes.

Best for: feature overviews, dashboards. Avoid when content is long-form.

### Pixel art

Retro 8-bit visuals, blocky graphics.

Best for: games, retro brands, indie tools. Avoid for modern SaaS.

### Conceptual sketch

Hand-drawn, sketch-like, loose lines.

Best for: early-stage, ideation, creative tools. Avoid for polished products.

### Luxury typography

Large serif headlines, generous spacing, restrained colour.

Best for: fashion, hospitality, high-end services. Avoid for dense interfaces.

### Editorial design

Magazine-style layouts, strong imagery, column grids.

Best for: content sites, blogs, publications. Avoid for app shells.

### Y2K aesthetic

Chrome, gradients, bubbly shapes, nostalgic 2000s.

Best for: Gen-Z brands, fashion, music. Avoid for B2B.

### Ethereal

Light, airy, soft gradients, floating elements.

Best for: wellness, beauty, lifestyle. Avoid for data-heavy products.

### Bohemian

Warm, earthy, eclectic, textured.

Best for: lifestyle, travel, artisan brands. Avoid for tech products.

### Dark mode UI

Dark surfaces, light text, accent glow.

Best for: developer tools, media, gaming. Avoid for print-like content.

### Cyberpunk

Neon, glitch, high contrast, dystopian.

Best for: gaming, tech events, niche brands. Avoid for mainstream products.

### Anthropomorphic

Human-like characters, mascots, expressive faces.

Best for: consumer apps, education. Avoid for enterprise.

### Victorian

Ornate, decorative, vintage, serif-heavy.

Best for: heritage, literary, niche brands. Avoid for modern tools.

### Cybercore

Cyberpunk-lite. Neon accents on dark surfaces.

Best for: tech, gaming, crypto. Avoid for mainstream.

### Synthwave

Retro-futuristic, neon grids, sunset palettes.

Best for: music, gaming, retro brands. Avoid for B2B.

### Graffiti

Urban, spray-paint, bold, street.

Best for: youth brands, streetwear, music. Avoid for corporate.

### Mixed media

Photos, illustration, texture, collage combined.

Best for: editorial, creative campaigns. Avoid for consistent UI.

### Wabi-sabi

Imperfect, natural, aged, quiet.

Best for: craft, wellness, slow brands. Avoid for tech-forward products.

## Avoid pairing

- Neumorphism with glassmorphism. Both are surface effects. Pick one.
- Brutalism with luxury typography. They fight.
- Bento grid with editorial. Grids clash with columns.
