# SOP: Avoid the Vibecoded Look

## What this skill owns

Thirty anti-patterns. Each one signals an AI-generated site. Avoid them, or fix them when you find them. This is a "do not" list, not a style guide. Pair it with ui/refero-design for positive direction.

## The anti-patterns

### 1. Harsh gradients

Bright multi-stop gradients scream template. Use flat color or a subtle two-stop gradient with low contrast.

### 2. Lucide icons

Lucide is the default AI icon set. Pick a different set, or use custom icons that match the brand.

### 3. Pure white background

Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) feels sterile. Use off-white or a warm neutral.

### 4. Rainbow colouring

More than two accent colours looks chaotic. Pick one accent and use neutrals for the rest.

### 5. Drop shadows

Heavy drop shadows look dated and cheap. Use borders, subtle elevation, or nothing.

### 6. Three feature cards in a row

The classic AI layout. Vary the layout: one big card, two small, or a list.

### 7. Emojis

Emojis in UI copy look unprofessional. Use words or icons instead.

### 8. Liquid glass

Glassmorphism everywhere is a trend, not a brand. Use it sparingly or skip it.

### 9. Em dashes

Em dashes in marketing copy are an AI tell. Use commas or periods.

### 10. Inter, Geist, or Space Grotesk

These are the default AI fonts. Pick a typeface with character that fits the brand.

### 11. Coloured left stripe

A coloured left border on a card is a common AI pattern. Use a full border or none.

### 12. Fake testimonials

Invented quotes with stock photos destroy trust. Use real ones or none.

### 13. Bento grids

Bento grids are overused. Use them only when the content earns the layout.

### 14. Terminal windows

Fake terminal screenshots look like every other AI landing page. Show the real product.

### 15. "It is not X, it is Y"

This sentence pattern is an AI tell. Write direct claims instead.

### 16. Checkmark bullets

Checkmark lists are generic. Use plain bullets or prose.

### 17. Three pricing tiers

Three tiers is the AI default. Use two or four if the product needs it.

### 18. No real product demos

Screenshots of dashboards are not demos. Show the product working.

### 19. Soft corner radius

Overly rounded corners look toy-like. Match radius to the brand.

### 20. Purple and black

Purple on black is the AI colour scheme. Pick a palette with intent.

### 21. No skeleton loaders

Missing loading states feel broken. Add skeletons for async content.

### 22. Radial orbs

Glowing orbs in the hero are an AI cliché. Remove them.

### 23. Dot grids

Dot-grid backgrounds are overused. Use solid colour or a subtle texture.

### 24. Sparkle icons

Sparkle icons signal "AI feature" generically. Use a specific icon.

### 25. Animated arrows

Bouncing arrows are a template tell. Use static or no arrows.

### 26. No terms of service

Missing legal pages look unfinished. Add TOS and privacy policy.

### 27. No privacy policy

Same as above. Required if you collect data.

### 28. Hover animations

Hover effects on everything feel noisy. Reserve them for interactive elements.

### 29. Neon colours

Neon accents look like a crypto site. Use muted or brand colours.

### 30. Basic pastel colours

Default pastels read as generic. Choose a deliberate palette.

## How to use this list

Run it as a review pass before launch. For each item, mark pass or fail. Fix failures before shipping. Pair with ui/refero-design for the positive direction.
