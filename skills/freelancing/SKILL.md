# Freelance Studio Playbook
### For developers operating as a small studio / collective

---

# CORE PHILOSOPHY

You are not a developer for hire. You are a problem-solving studio that happens to write code.

• Sell outcomes, not hours  
• Document everything: scope, deliverables, timelines, payments  
• Under-promise, over-deliver  
• Start as individuals, present as a team  

---

# 0. POSITIONING -- Agency vs Individual vs Studio

### The Right Answer: "Small Studio"

Do not say: "I'm a freelancer"  
Do not say: "We're an agency"  
Do say: "We're a small product studio" or "We're a boutique dev team"

Benefits:

• Trust of a team  
• Agility of freelancers  
• Premium feel without large-agency pricing  

---

# 1. FINDING AND EVALUATING CLIENTS

## Platform Strategy

### Upwork
• Complete profile fully  
• Start with small jobs ($200 to $500) for reviews  
• Apply selectively  
• Conversion improves after 5+ reviews  

### Fiverr
• Create specific gigs
• Offer upsells
• Fast delivery builds traction

### Direct Outreach
LinkedIn founders, startups via Crunchbase, YC directory.

### Referrals
Offer 5-10% referral fee.

---

## Red Flags

🚩 Pay after funding  
🚩 Vague scope + urgent deadline  
🚩 Free test work beyond 30 min  
🚩 Refuses contract  
🚩 Constant scope changes early  

---

## Green Flags

✅ Clear requirements  
✅ Budget mentioned  
✅ Responsive communication  
✅ Prior experience hiring developers  
✅ Accepts scoping call
