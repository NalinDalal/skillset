# Further Reading (only surface if user wants deeper/book-level material)

## Essays (free, short, high-signal)

- Paul Graham: "How to Get Startup Ideas", "Do Things That Don't Scale", "18 Mistakes That Kill Startups", "How to Raise Money", "Maker's Schedule, Manager's Schedule", paulgraham.com

- Y Combinator: "A Guide to Seed Fundraising", ycombinator.com/library

- Sam Altman: "Startup Playbook", blog.samaltman.com

## Books (by topic)

- **Idea/validation**: The Mom Test (Rob Fitzpatrick), The Lean Startup (Eric Ries), Running Lean (Ash Maurya)

- **Strategy/building**: Zero to One (Peter Thiel), The Hard Thing About Hard Things (Ben Horowitz), Disciplined Entrepreneurship (Bill Aulet)

- **Product**: Inspired / Empowered (Marty Cagan), The Cold Start Problem (Andrew Chen)

- **Fundraising**: Venture Deals (Brad Feld & Jason Mendelson)

- **Founder mental health/productivity**: Atomic Habits (James Clear), Thinking Fast and Slow (Kahneman)

## Communities worth lurking in

Indie Hackers, r/startups, r/SaaS, Hacker News, good for both learning and eventually launching.
