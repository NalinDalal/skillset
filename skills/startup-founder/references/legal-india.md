# Legal & Registration — India

## When to register at all
Don't register until you have: a paying customer, a co-founder needing formal equity, or you're about to raise money / sign a real contract / open a business bank account. Before that, build and validate as an individual — no need to spend money on incorporation for an unvalidated idea.

## Entity types
- **Sole Proprietorship**: fastest, no separate legal entity, unlimited personal liability, fine for freelancing/service work under one person (fits nerdev's current studio model). Just needs GST registration (if turnover crosses threshold) and a current account.
- **LLP (Limited Liability Partnership)**: liability protection, cheaper compliance than Pvt Ltd, works if 2+ founders and not planning to raise VC money (VCs generally won't invest in LLPs).
- **Private Limited Company (Pvt Ltd)**: the standard choice if planning to raise funding, hire formally, or want limited liability + credibility with enterprise clients. Required structure for most Indian VC/angel investment.

## Pvt Ltd registration — rough steps
1. Get Digital Signature Certificate (DSC) for all directors.
2. Apply for Director Identification Number (DIN).
3. Reserve company name via MCA's RUN/SPICe+ service.
4. File SPICe+ (integrated form: incorporation, PAN, TAN, EPFO, ESIC, GST registration all in one).
5. Draft MOA (Memorandum) and AOA (Articles of Association).
6. Get Certificate of Incorporation from MCA.
7. Open a company bank account, get PAN/TAN linked.
Realistic timeline: 1-2 weeks if documents are ready. Cost: roughly ₹6,000-15,000 in government + professional fees for a simple 2-founder company (use a CA or a service like Vakilsearch/IndiaFilings/ClearTax rather than DIY for a first-timer).

## GST
- Mandatory once turnover crosses ₹20 lakh (services) / ₹40 lakh (goods) in most states — lower thresholds in some special-category states.
- Needed earlier if selling to businesses that require GST invoices, or selling across state lines in some cases.
- Freelance/dev services to foreign clients: check "export of services" GST rules (usually zero-rated, but registration + LUT filing still required above threshold).

## Startup India recognition (DPIIT)
- Apply after incorporation via the Startup India portal.
- Benefits: income tax exemption (3 of first 10 years, subject to conditions), easier compliance, access to Startup India Seed Fund Scheme, fast-track patent examination.
- Requirements broadly: incorporated <10 years ago, turnover <₹100 crore, working on innovation/improvement of products or a scalable business model.

## Co-founder agreements
- Even for 2 friends starting together: write a founders' agreement covering equity split, vesting (standard: 4 years, 1-year cliff), roles, IP assignment (all IP built for the company belongs to the company, not the individual), and what happens if someone leaves early.
- Do this before writing significant product code together — cheap to do early, expensive/painful to fix later.

## Notes
- Rules and thresholds change — verify current numbers (GST thresholds, Startup India criteria, fees) via a web search or a CA before relying on exact figures here.
